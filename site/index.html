            SIX / 010QUESTIONS

            Straight
            answers.

            What are you raising money for?

            The new Smartan House facility and the Acorn Incubator Hub. Nothing else — this campaign funds one facility.

            How much?

            ₦300,000,000 — 30,000 bricks of ₦10,000 each.

            Why?

            To expand Smartan's ability to support exceptional young people, particularly those from underserved communities, with a permanent home to live, learn and launch.

            Where does the money go?

            Into the facility, following Smartan's approved project budget. You can see the allocation and every verified entry in the transparency ledger.

            How much has been raised?

            The live, verified figure is at the top of this page: ₦0 of ₦300,000,000.

            What happens after I contribute?

            You become a Builder and your bricks are laid on the wall. You receive a Builder ID, your Impact Units and a digital receipt — plus a Builder card you can share.

            What is the minimum?

            ₦10,000 — one brick. There is no maximum.

            Do I need an account?

            No. Choose an amount, add your name and email, and pay. That's it.

            Is this an investment?

            No. Impact Units are campaign contribution units, not financial securities. They carry no ownership, dividends, returns or resale value.
