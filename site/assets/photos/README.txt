facility.webp is the facility render: shown in the hero (js/config.js -> facility.model) and as photo 04.

Put the other campaign photographs here, then set each one's src (and alt text) in js/config.js -> photos.

01 case-work.webp          Young people at work in the current space (candid, landscape)
02 case-space.webp         The current space, showing it is full or outgrown
03 case-mentor.webp        A mentoring moment, one or two people, close up
04 facility.webp         Architect's render, exterior (in use)
05 facility-site.webp      The site today, clear daylight photo
06 facility-acorn.webp     Render of the Acorn Incubator Hub interior
07 facility-plan.webp      Floor plan with spaces labelled
08 site-latest.webp        Latest dated photo from the site (replace at each milestone)
09 founder.webp            Founder or leadership portrait (portrait orientation)

Tips: WebP, 2400px wide for 01/04/08, 1600px for the rest, under ~300 KB each.
Real images only. Photos of young people need consent (and a guardian's for under-18s).
